This screen recording shows the animated Blender camera view beside the generated result. The players are not precisely synchronized. Its title bar is masked, audio removed and recording metadata stripped for public sharing.

What the demonstration establishes: Blender defines the guide's camera paths, cuts and spatial staging. Seedance interprets those rendered pixels to generate the finished appearance. The model still influences the output: correspondence is approximate, not a guarantee of identical camera motion, geometry or object counts.

Reproduce the guide

Download the editable Blender scene · Scene build script

Open the scene at frame 1 and switch to Camera Perspective (Numpad 0). The file opens in camera view in this public edition. The .blend is the greybox guide; the photoreal scene is generated video, not reconstructed editable geometry.

blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python build_perfume_world.py

This builds the scene and preview images locally. Add -- --render to render the complete guide sequence, then run python production/encode_guides.py. Cloud generation requires your own Higgsfield account and a new paid job; no credential is distributed here.
